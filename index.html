About ShiftMate

ShiftMate is a free collection of practical tools created by Prabhat.

Privacy

No analytics, advertising, remote APIs, accounts, or file uploads are included. Calculations and CSV inspection run in this browser. Inputs are not intentionally saved to persistent browser storage. Browser extensions and device-level software operate independently.

Accuracy and limitations

Results depend on your inputs and assumptions. Verify important results independently. The salary estimator covers one shift only and does not calculate tax, statutory deductions, or legal entitlements. CSV is supported; XLSX is not. The writing helper uses static templates.
